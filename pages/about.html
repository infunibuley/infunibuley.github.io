About this thing

So what is this you might ask. I'm also asking that. I'm perpetually confused.

Basically infunibuley is my record of every single dumb question being answered live. Why do people hate the idea of pineapple on pizza? Why has food delivery become so popular? What defines different genders? What are people's favorite colors and why?

And hopefully this will also be for other people's questions (hence the suggestions box on the home page).

This is me using what I learned about tech, data, computers, privacy, research, and community to answer questions like are hot dogs tacos or sandwiches.

And for this, I hope you stay and have a time of your life here. Maybe not the time, but it'll definitely be a time.

Transparency and Anonymity

And with this, I do hope that I can be as transparent as possible. I'm using github because it's free. I am doing everything on my own and none of your information will be shared outside. Your anonymity is what I care most about. I will never require your name, phone-number, email, or anything else that could link to you, and if I do, please scold me in my email (click contact at the bottom). It was probably a coding mishap.

If I ever do ask for your email, it's only if you want me to notify you on an update. I will never be creepy and try to find you. I quite literally do not care.

Also, if you want me to share anything about how I'm doing anything, please let me know and I will share!

Guide to za projects

The Stages

This is sort of roughly how each of the projects will be formatted. Each project will also have an updated tag with these names on them.

1. mind works

This is basically the planning portion of the project.

In this stage where I'm asking the initial question. I'm making hypotheses. I'm thinking about how I could find answers, what might come in the way, what I should be worrying about.

Here I'm writing up a game plan in messy notes.

2. the gears

The gears will include readings on news, research papers, and more about the topic. It's basically a literature review

It'll also include a solidified plan for this project, with a timeline of when I'll be posting and closing surveys, when I'll be doing the analysis, and when I'll be posting my conclusions.

3. your sticks

Your sticks are the responses to survey. When a project has this tag, it means the survey is currently open! It's when you get to contribute, when the actual important stuff happens!

4. fancy stuff

This is the data analysis. It'll have anything I was able to find with the data, conclusions, hopefully answers to my questions, new questions, future projects than could happen with this information, or more. Who knows!

When this tag is on a project, it's because the survey is closed and I'm working on all this!

5. complete

Go figure.

This tag will also mean that the analysis and stuff is posted.

Thanks for reading and I hope you enjoy what exists and what's to come!
