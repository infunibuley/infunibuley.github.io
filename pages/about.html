About meh

Welcome, visitor, to my humble abode. or something.

Idk man, I'm workin on the greetings.

For like years, I've had ideas. Mostly because of some classes I took back in college.

Weird thing, I enjoyed college quite a bit. Mostly the friends, the shenanigans, the tomfoolery. Sure also the parties, the crazy dress codes, the stupidly long discussions that made it to 7 in the morning when we all had 9ams.

And another weird thing, I enjoyed the classes. I'd always wanted to do something with my life. Help people. Be someone for something. Do something. But I didn't know how. Until I took Research Design.

God was that a fun class. With projects and gadgets and gizmos and whatnot. But you know the other class that brought it together? Data Science

And good lord was that a torturous class.

But it taught me more than I thought. And way less. It taught me I can learn even after college (my passion apparently is learning. Boring, I know) in a way that I can help people. There's health data, census data, edu data, and way more that could make a change.

Slight issue, I'm unemployed. I graduated a few months before deciding this is what I'm going to start during my grand unemployment. So until I find a job, y'all have to deal with meh!

I do have to give credits to 1 person other than my friends. There was a professor that I'd appreciated since the first class I had taken with him all the way until the 3rd, when I was practically at his feet asking for a D so I could graduate. He taught me about the community, about thinking deeper. I'll probably disappoint him with this, but I do want to at least try to use this to inform people, to tell the stories that get hidden by trends, stereotypes, the crazy polarity in opinions society carries, and other mishaps with our wonderful society. Even if I don't make him proud, at least my fumbling will hopefully be entertaining to you.

Thank you for joining me on this journey.

Thanks for reading and I hope you enjoy what exists and what's to come!
