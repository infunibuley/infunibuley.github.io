## 1. Napkin Blueprints

I keep seeing everyone around me using AI (I'm guilty as well), for simple things like trying to find the meaning of a word or answering algebra problems. 

Or I see people use it for opinions, validation, ideas. 

But the internet was built for human-human connection, not GenAI-human connection. 

I wonder what people use GenAI for and why, whether it's for technical utility, creativity or intellectual collaboration, general searches, or something else. 

I want to know if to find different answers, do people rely first on GenAI, the connective internet, or something else? 

This project will have a survey. 

Some things I might look into are probably how many close friends people have and what they're comfortable asking their friends versus what they lean more on GenAI for. 

I might look into how old the person is and when they remember that they started using GenAI, and how often they use it now. 

I might also ask how they feel about the use of AI, the building of data centers, and the environmental impacts of AI. Does a lower inclination towards AI really affect the use of it? I think that could help indicate whether it's an addiction or not. Sure, I don't like the addictive nature and dopamine-focused content on social media (while I also understand the positive impacts), yet I still watch it and scroll for hours at a time. 

The survey will likely have: 

- Demographics + approx. number of close friends/acquaintances
- Use cases
- Mini open-internet quiz
- Ethical concerns rating vs frequency of use
- Other thoughts on AI, the future, future research, future
