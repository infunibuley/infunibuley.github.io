# infunibuley

Infunibuley is a blog-ish fun site just for me to post some independent projects which some might call research, but I call touching data. My hope is that I can eventually get good enough to make surveys to find real data that changes the way we see the people around us (hopefully for the better, I'm an optimist/realist).

<br>

## About *this thing*

So what is this you might ask. I'm also asking that. I'm perpetually confused. 

Basically **infunibuley** is my record of every single dumb question being answered live. Why do people hate the idea of pineapple on pizza? Why has food delivery become so popular? What defines different genders? What are people's favorite colors and why? 

And hopefully this will also be for other people's questions (hence the [suggestions box](https://infunibuley.github.io/pages/contact "Contact Page, drop a suggestion!"))

This is me using what I learned about tech, data, computers, privacy, research, and community to answer questions like are hot dogs tacos or sandwiches.

And for this, I hope you stay and have a time of your life here. Maybe not *the* time, but it'll definitely be *a* time.

## Guide to *za projects*

### The Stages

This is sort of roughly how each of the projects will be formatted. Each project will also have an updated tag with these names on them.

#### 1. Napkin Blueprints

**The Vibe:** *sketches on napkin*

Datasets I wanna look at, dumb questions, dumber hypotheses, and overall just a brain dump.

#### 2. Ancient Scrolls

**The Vibe:** *digging through the archives*
 
A simple lit review when I feel like not reinventing the wheel or learning more context first.

#### 3. Calling all Echoes

**The Vibe:** *let me dig into your brains*

Survey and Data-gathering is active when needed! A lot of the time there might be a following project to a project without a survey that'll end up having a survey! Did that English?

#### 4. Cranking the Dials

**The Vibe:** *crunching numbers and the cybery lookin stuff (kind of)*

EDA and data analysis sometimes. I'm bigger on looking around at the data and trying to find discrepancies than making predictive models.

#### 5. The Final Verdict
**The Vibe:** *describing the taste of the water, sealing the case, and getting straight to the point*
Discussion of what the data shows, final conclusions, any concerns (aaah the world is scary!), and closing out the question.

### Transparency and Anonymity

And with this, I do hope that I can be as transparent as possible. I'm using github because it's free. I am doing everything on my own and none of your information will be shared outside. Your anonymity is what I care most about. I will never require your name, phone-number, email, or anything else that could link to you, and if I do, please scold me in my email (infunibuley@proton.me). It was probably a coding mishap or oversight.

If I ever do ask for your email, it's only if you want me to notify you on an update. I will never be creepy and try to find you. I quite literally do not care. My goal is to find patterns in the data, understand society. Not to find you? So I don't know.

Again, [contact me](https://infunibuley.github.io/pages/contact "Contact Page, drop a suggestion!") if you're ever worried I'm out to find you and I'll do my best to make sure I can't in any way. 

Also, if you want me to share anything about how I'm doing anything, please let me know and I will share!
